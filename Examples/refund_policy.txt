Refund policy

1. Window. A customer may return any item within 30 days of delivery for a
   full refund.

2. Condition. The item must be unused and in its original packaging with all
   parts. We inspect every returned item when it arrives.

3. How to return. Support sends a prepaid return label and the customer drops
   the package at any carrier location. Return shipping is free for a
   defective or incorrect item. For a change of mind, a flat $8 comes off
   the refund.

4. Refund timing. We refund to the original payment method within 5 business
   days of receiving the item. The customer's bank may take 3 to 5 business
   days more to post it.

5. Store credit. On request, we issue store credit as soon as the return
   label is created, so the customer can place a new order without waiting
   for the refund.

6. Exclusions. Final sale items, gift cards, and items damaged by misuse are
   not refundable.

7. Damage in transit. An item damaged in transit is replaced at no cost.
   Shipping handles the claim with the carrier; the customer keeps or
   discards the damaged item as we instruct.

8. Exchanges. An exchange is a return plus a new order. The new order is
   charged in full and the returned item is refunded under this policy.

9. Silence. Where this policy does not cover a case, the answer is no refund
   without a manager's approval.
